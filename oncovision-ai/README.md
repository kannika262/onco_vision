# OncoVision AI 🧠⚕️
## AI-Powered Early Cancer Detection & Medical Imaging Platform

OncoVision AI is an advanced AI-powered healthcare platform designed for early cancer detection using medical imaging and deep learning.  
The platform enables patients, doctors, hospitals, and diagnostic centers to upload medical scans and receive intelligent AI-assisted diagnostic insights.

The system integrates:
- Deep Learning Models
- Medical Image Processing
- Tumor Detection
- Explainable AI Heatmaps
- Risk Analysis
- Doctor Verification Workflow
- AI Report Generation
- research papers suggestion
---
